# Look C — review of plan against the skill's generic looks (before build)

Short talk. "Absent" = not in study. "Deliberate" = in study, on purpose, reason given.

## Five generic looks
1. Cream page + contrast serif + clay accent: ABSENT. Page `#FFFFFF` (correction 2). No serif at all (correction 3). No accent hue; nearest is dark seat 4 `#DE6365`, one seat of seven.
2. Near-black page + one acid accent: ABSENT. Dark page `#151B18` has no single accent; seven seats, none acid, controls pale ink.
3. Broadsheet: hairline rules, zero radius, dense columns: ABSENT in the app. Two hairlines only (under top bar, beside sidebar). Study frame itself is square-cornered: it is a window edge, not a card.
4. Card kit: same rounded cards, one radius, same grey shadow, gradient wash: ABSENT. No shadow anywhere (correction 6). No gradient. Three corner sizes by role. Conversation has no cards except bubbles, message box, folded card.
5. Template chrome:
   - ALL-CAPS tracked eyebrow: ABSENT.
   - Middle dots between facts: ABSENT. Pill parts split by gaps (correction 5).
   - "WORD — fragment" labels: ABSENT.
   - Tinted near-black standing in for black: DELIBERATE. Ink `#17201C` is the direction's written text colour; builder may not swap.
   - Mono face for small numbers: ABSENT. Archivo tabular figures.
   - Arrow on link or button text: ABSENT.

## Type tells
- One word of a headline in italic, bold or colour: ABSENT. In panel 4 pill the word "Agreed" is green with a check: that is the agreed mark (direction: colour + check + word), not a headline accent. DELIBERATE.
- All caps for labels: ABSENT.
- Needless labels above content: one label, "Files cited", is in the shared content and names a real group. DELIBERATE. "Time limit 5:00" under the question is a fact from the shared content, not an eyebrow.
- Numbered markers 01 / 02 / 03: ABSENT. Panel captions carry no numbers (four views, not steps).
- Scattered motion: ABSENT. One bubble fade on Play. No hover lifts, no entrances, no loops.

## Still the default? Checked by working a look-alike brief ("group chat with avatars and a meter")

- Weak spot kept honest: head + half-circle body IS the stock user-icon outline, and shared brief fixes body at 1.4 D wide. Correction 15 wants narrow shoulders. Cannot change frozen size. Worked round with what is left: name fills head, cut in body, no outline, no face. Reported.

## Revisions to how the study is DRAWN (plan stage)
1. Message box: direction says table diameter = message box width (640). Shared sizes give table 442 (five) and 466 (seven). Same-width box under a half-circle draws a tombstone. Changed: box stays 640 on the centre line in all four panels, table sits on its top edge. Reason: no tombstone, box does not jump between panels, answer column keeps 640.
2. Stage footer: Pause, Stop (left) and Table / Chat (right) drawn on the message-box row, not a separate row under the stage. Reason: one base line under the table instead of two rows of controls; saves 44 px for air above the bubbles.
3. Bubble gap: 12 px measured from the top of what is drawn (head, or its ring when speaking). Reason: with gap from head only, the bubble tail pokes into the speaker ring.
4. Bubbles: shrink-wrapped to their two balanced lines and anchored left, right or centre with the tail under their own head. Reason: five bubbles at once fit without one rising over another.
5. Speaker posture: 4 px lean and 2 px head rise drawn as fixed offsets (correction 7 keeps the lean). Too small to count on; ring is the mark.
6. Surface light: no tint step. Reason: on white, a grey fill under bubbles reads as "disabled"; the 3.99:1 edge already separates.
7. Dropped before build (mirror check): "5 seated" text in top bar (title already says it, names are written); attach icon in message box; small Phi figure beside the dissent line (line-up directly above already shows Phi with a cross).

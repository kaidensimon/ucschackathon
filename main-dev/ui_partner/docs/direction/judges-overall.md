Show all three, after the fixes. None should go to a study as written.

### Too similar
- All three light pages are the same colour to the eye: room #E9ECE8, record #E5ECE4, voices #F1F2EE. Computed OKLab distances are 0.8 (room and record), 2.0 (room and voices) and 2.5 (record and voices), where about 2 is just noticeable. The dark pages are 2.3 to 3.0 apart. - 'room' and 'voices' share the same layout idea: the table's flat side docked on the message box at the same width ('you have the chair' and 'your place at the table'), no accent colour, an ink main button, and solid dark figures with pale names in light theme and pale figures in dark.
- 'room' and 'voices' share the same agreement meter: a row of 12 px discs or pips, filled for agree and hollow for not, with a notch after the number needed.
- All three use the same vote marks (a solid ink check disc and a hollow ink cross disc, 18 to 22 px, at the head's upper right), near-identical agreed greens (#2A6A43, #136239, #1A6B42) and error reds (#A52A1A, #A12628, #A32A1C), the same split between a 3:1 outline colour and a decorative rule (#717A75, #717E76, #77827B), a 48 px top bar, a 232 px sidebar, 'radius by role', tabular figures in place of a monospace, and the same wording rules.
- Net effect: outside the table and figures, the three differ mainly by typeface. If the owner is to choose between three real alternatives, at least one should move its page off grey-green and 'voices' or 'record' should change its type pair.

### Recommendation
Put 'room' (The Lit Table) first: it is the only one with a single picture the owner can judge by eye in a second, a dark table where a lamp comes on in front of whoever is speaking, it keeps the warmth he first chose, and every fault I found in it is a cheap correction to the written spec (fill the vote discs, add an ink ring on the speaking head, reconcile the stage widths, set a minimum column width) and not a flaw in the idea. 'voices' is the safest fallback, with the fewest misses and the best colour-blind separation (worst pair 8.2 in light, 11.4 in dark), but it is the least distinctive and shares its two typefaces with 'record'. Show 'record' third and only after its hatches are proven at ordinary screen sharpness, because it carries the most unproven function (colour only in 2 px lines, a focus ring identical to the speaker ring, columns that never appear with five seats), and before any study is drawn push the three page colours apart, since today they are 0.8 to 2.5 apart and will look like the same page.

## Other skill checks

- Motion: one bubble on Play (asked). No loop in study. No hover lift.
- Big number + small label hero: absent. Count is strokes; timer is 20 px.
- Similar-prompt test ("AI debate app"): would give solid colour avatars, tailed chat bubbles, progress bar, glow. Here: ringed pale heads, slips with stem line, strokes, second ring. ("Minutes app": cream, Courier, red margin, stamp. None here.)

## What I changed in the plan before build (and why)
- Send button: violet -> ink (correction 2). Violet now only speaker ring + active switch side.
- Focus: violet head ring -> ink box round whole figure (correction 3). I use ink for every focus ring, so violet never means "focused".
- Vote mark: both-pale discs -> check on solid ink disc, cross on pale disc (correction 7). Added 2 px page-colour halo round disc: ink disc straight on sepia ring would be 1.47:1.
- Bubble edge: rule -> outline (correction 6).
- Top bar: direction wanted five names with tiny hatch chips. Dropped before build: brief gives "Council of 5" only, and judges said too many devices. Fewer things fight the tally.
- Helper line under message box ("The council reads this at the start of the next round.") dropped: placeholder "Add a note for the next round" already says it.

## Revisions to how study is DRAWN (after looking at shots)
1. Dots pattern redrawn. First: two dots per 6 x 6 tile, read as dark mesh, same as grid seat at scale 1. Now: rows 6 px apart, each next row shifted 3 px. Reason: seats 3 and 7 must not look alike.
2. Pattern lines moved to whole pixels (centre 2.75). Reason: soft at ordinary sharpness. Pitch stays 6 px; no need for 8.
3. Margin line in panel 4 cut at the docket. Reason: it ran through empty page and poked out between docket and sheet. This is the "remove one thing".
4. Speaker ring 3 px, 4 px gap, small bite out of body under it. Reason: shared 12 px bubble gap; 2.5 px blurs at scale 1; violet must not touch seat colour.
5. Vote disc halo 2 px in page colour. Reason: ink on seat ring fails 3:1.
